## About Augusta Heritage Center

Augusta Heritage Center is a nonprofit arts and culture organization headquartered in Elkins, West Virginia. It preserves and teaches traditional Appalachian arts — old-time and bluegrass music, dance, folklore, craft, and luthiery — through immersive workshop weeks held each summer.

- **Location:** Elkins, WV (Randolph County)
- **Mission:** Preserve America's traditional music and folk arts; teach the next generation through hands-on workshops
- **Annual revenue (FY 2023):** $1,395,069
- **Public support:** 99.91% (grants and contributions)
- **Anchor asset:** The Wilt Building, Augusta's commercial space in downtown Elkins
- **Executive Director:** Seth Young (seth@augustaartsandculture.org)
- **Website:** augustaartsandculture.org

## Signature programs — themed workshop weeks

Augusta's flagship offering is its themed weeks: each week, dozens of master artists teach hundreds of students in intensive small-group classes. Typical 2025–2026 weeks include:

- **Bluegrass Week** — fiddle, banjo, mandolin, guitar, bass, vocals
- **Old-Time Week** — clawhammer banjo, fiddle, traditional songs
- **Classic Country Week** — honky-tonk, swing, vintage Nashville styles
- **Cajun & Creole Week** — accordion, fiddle, French language, cooking
- **Blues Week** — guitar, harmonica, piano, history
- **Vocal Week** — harmony singing, ballad, gospel
- **Dance Week** — flatfoot, contra, square, clogging
- **Folk Arts Week** — basketry, weaving, blacksmithing, woodcarving, pottery
- **Early Country & Bluegrass Week** — pre-war styles, Carter Family, Bill Monroe

Each week includes daily classes, evening concerts open to the public, jam sessions, dances, and a culminating student showcase.

## Who attends Augusta

- **Students:** Adults of all skill levels, from absolute beginner to professional. Many are repeat attendees ("third Classic Country Week" is common).
- **Faculty:** Touring musicians, master craft artists, ethnomusicologists.
- **Public audiences:** Evening concerts and weekend events are open to anyone in Elkins.

## Public events open to tourists

Beyond the workshops themselves, Augusta hosts several events tourists can attend without enrolling:

- **Evening concerts** during each themed week (usually Wed–Fri, ~7pm)
- **Student showcases** (Friday evenings)
- **Open dances** — contra and square dances most weekends during summer
- **Free midday concerts** on the Davis & Elkins College campus

## Other Elkins/Randolph County arts organizations & artists

Augusta is the anchor, but a wider arts ecosystem surrounds it. The bot should mention these when relevant:

### Visual artists & studios

- **Kevin Woodcock** — Painter, traditional and Appalachian landscapes. Website: woodcockart.com. Instagram active.
- **Rosalie Haizlett** — Nature illustrator, watercolor and pen, focuses on Appalachian flora and fauna. Sells prints, books, Skillshare classes. Most prolific online presence of any local artist.
- **Seneca Designs** — Hand-crafted jewelry and giftware inspired by West Virginia landscapes. Based in Randolph County.

### Galleries & studios

- **The Arts Center** — Multi-disciplinary community arts center in downtown Elkins. Classes, gallery space, exhibitions.
- **Artists at Work** — Co-op gallery and studio space showcasing 20+ regional artists across painting, pottery, jewelry, fiber, and more.
- **Ceramics with Class** — Pottery studio offering classes and open studio time. Elkins, WV.
- **Elkins Sewing Center** — Fabric, sewing classes, quilting community hub since 1982.

### Performance venues

- **The Gandy Dancer Theatre** — Historic theater in downtown Elkins, community theater productions year-round.
- **Old Brick Playhouse** — Youth theater company and venue.
- **Davis & Elkins College Performing Arts Series** — Visiting national acts at Halliehurst Hall.

### Recurring community events

- **Pickin' in the Park** — Free outdoor bluegrass jam at City Park (Elkins-Randolph County Tourism organizes; usually Tuesday evenings in summer).
- **Mountain State Forest Festival** — Multi-day fall festival, parades, music, crafts (early October).
- **First Friday Art Walks** — Downtown galleries and studios open late, music in the streets (first Friday of each month).
- **Augusta concerts** — see above.

## Day-trip suggestions for visitors

If a tourist is in Elkins for an Augusta event and has half a day to explore, recommend:

- **Blackwater Falls State Park** (~45 min drive) — iconic WV waterfall, hiking
- **Seneca Rocks & Spruce Knob** (~1 hr) — climbing, the highest peak in WV
- **Cass Scenic Railroad** (~1 hr) — historic steam train through mountains
- **Snowshoe Mountain** (~1 hr) — summer mountain biking, scenic chairlift rides
- **Monongahela National Forest** (surrounds Elkins) — hiking, trout fishing, scenic drives
- **Downtown Elkins walking tour** — historic train depot, Tygart River, courthouse square
